EXPERTISE

Transforming Existing Spaces

Working within existing constraints is always a challenge, but also a significant opportunity. The cost savings and reduced embodied carbon that result are a welcome win. This is an increasingly common need in today's world, particularly when it comes to the reuse of buildings from the 1960s and 1970s.

From the Ground Up

At times, a new building is needed to meet the needs of a program — whether an addition or a full campus. Site planning, campus integration, and long-term flexibility all become central considerations, ensuring the building serves not only today's needs but those of the years ahead. Expertise listed below reflects projects personally led.

The Unsaid Experience

Interior Design and Interior Architecture go hand in hand. When material choices are limited by budget or cleanability requirements, architecture becomes essential. How a space feels, its scale, and its approachability are just as important as the finishes. Together, these elements create the space the end user truly needs.

About

Sixteen years of architecture. Ten-plus in labs.

Allison Ciafardini has 16 years of work experience in the architectural industry across the United States. Her career began in Atlanta after the Great Recession, a period that brought significant opportunity as the city grew quickly.

Twelve years were spent working alongside colleagues at Cooper Carry, a period that offered substantial growth in both experience and relationships. That growth spanned leading small restroom renovations independently to overseeing multimillion-dollar high-rise projects. The relationships and projects built during that time remain a source of great pride.

As her career progressed, focus shifted increasingly toward lab design. Conversations with principal investigators and the challenge of designing spaces tailored to their needs became a central interest. This led to a transition into a Lab Planning–focused role at Flad Architects, leading lab planning efforts on projects large and small across the country. Providing for researchers' needs consistently proved to be the most rewarding part of the work.

Now at mid-career, that same focus — creating space for individual needs — has become the foundation of Whitecap Architecture, which offers detailed design and full services tailored to small-scale projects. Expertise in complex lab design and coordination remains central to the practice. Please visit the projects page for references and examples of past work.
